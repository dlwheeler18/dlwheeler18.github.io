Breakfast
Mango & Cardamom Oats
×2
80g rolled oats soaked overnight in Plenish oat milk with ground cardamom, topped with frozen mango chunks and a drizzle of honey. Serve with a pot of kefir on the side.
🫙 Kefir Oats · Kefir
Measure 80g rolled oats (split between 2 bowls: 40g each). Add 40ml Plenish oat milk to each bowl, plus a pinch of ground cardamom to taste.
Cover and refrigerate overnight, or for at least 4 hours.
In the morning, top each bowl with a handful of frozen mango chunks (thaw as you eat or add hot water to warm them). Drizzle with honey.
Serve with a small pot of kefir on the side to drink or stir in.
per person
~370 kcal

Lunch
Butter Bean & Roasted Pepper Salad
×2
Butter beans, jarred roasted red peppers, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and flat-leaf parsley tossed in a sherry vinegar and olive oil dressing. Served with a thick slice of sourdough.
🇪🇸 Spanish Butter beans 5 min assembly
Drain and rinse 1 tin butter beans. Roughly chop 2 jarred roasted red peppers.
In a bowl, combine butter beans, peppers, a handful of cherry tomatoes (halved), ¼ thinly sliced red onion, and a generous handful of flat-leaf parsley (roughly chopped).
Dress with 2 tbsp sherry vinegar, 4 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper. Toss well.
Divide between 2 bowls. Serve each with a thick slice of sourdough.
per person
~390 kcal

Dinner
Romesco Chicken
×2
Pan-seared chicken breast served with homemade romesco — roasted red peppers, blanched almonds, tomato, garlic, and smoked paprika blended to a rich, silky sauce. Served over wilted spinach with sourdough to mop up.
🇪🇸 Spanish Chicken breast
Blend 2 jarred roasted red peppers, 30g blanched almonds, 1 small tomato, 1 garlic clove, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tbsp sherry vinegar, 3 tbsp olive oil. Season well and set aside.
Season 2 chicken breasts with salt and pepper. Heat oil in an ovenproof frying pan over high heat. Sear chicken 3 min each side until golden.
Pour romesco sauce around the chicken. Transfer to oven at 190°C for 15–18 min until cooked through. Repeat for second portion.
Wilt a large handful of spinach per person in a dry pan with a tiny bit of garlic. Season. Serve chicken over spinach, sauce spooned generously over the top, sourdough alongside.
per person
~490 kcal

Blueberry & Vanilla Oats
×1
50g rolled oats soaked overnight in Plenish oat milk with a splash of vanilla extract. Topped with frozen blueberries and a spoonful of live yoghurt.
🫙 Live yoghurt Oats · Yoghurt
Measure 50g rolled oats into a bowl. Add 250ml Plenish oat milk and a splash of pure vanilla extract.

In the morning, top with a handful of frozen blueberries (thawed or straight from the freezer). Add a generous spoonful of live yoghurt.
Stir gently or eat as layered. Enjoy cold or warm it briefly if preferred.

Lunch
Red Lentil & Roasted Pepper Soup
×1
Silky, warmly spiced soup of red lentils and roasted red peppers with cumin, smoked paprika, garlic, and a squeeze of lemon. Thick and filling — served with a slice of sourdough. Can be batch-made and frozen.
🌍 Middle Eastern Red lentils Freeze-friendly
Soften ½ onion and 1 garlic clove in olive oil over medium heat, 5 min.
Add 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp smoked paprika. Stir 1 min until fragrant.
Add 60g red lentils, 1 jarred roasted red pepper (roughly chopped), and 450ml vegetable stock. Simmer 20 min until lentils are completely soft.
Blend until smooth with a stick blender. Squeeze in lemon juice, season well. Serve with sourdough.

Dinner
Shakshuka
×1
Eggs poached in a rich, spiced tomato and pepper sauce with cumin, smoked paprika, harissa, garlic, and onion. A little crumbled feta on top, fresh parsley, and sourdough to scoop.
🌍 Middle Eastern Eggs One pan · 25 min
Heat olive oil in a wide frying pan. Soften ½ onion and 1 red pepper, sliced, for 8 min over medium heat.
Add 2 garlic cloves (minced), 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp harissa. Cook 1 min.
Add 1 tin chopped tomatoes and a splash of water. Season well. Simmer 10 min until sauce is thick and glossy.
Make 2 wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each. Cover the pan and cook 5–6 min until whites are set but yolks still soft.
Crumble a little feta over the top, scatter with flat-leaf parsley. Serve straight from the pan with sourdough.
per person
~450 kcal

Apple, Cinnamon & Walnut Oats
×1
50g rolled oats soaked overnight in Plenish oat milk with cinnamon and 1 tbsp ground flaxseed. Topped with grated apple, a small handful of walnuts, and a drizzle of honey.
Oats · Walnuts · Flaxseed ♥ Cholesterol support
Measure 50g rolled oats into a bowl. Add 250ml Plenish oat milk, a pinch of ground cinnamon, and 1 tbsp ground flaxseed.
Cover and refrigerate overnight, or for at least 4 hours.
In the morning, grate a fresh apple over the top. Scatter a small handful of walnuts (about 15g) and drizzle with a little honey.
Eat cold or gently warm in the microwave. The flaxseed and walnuts provide omega-3s for cholesterol support.

×1
Black beans, sweetcorn, red pepper, red onion, and cherry tomatoes with a cumin and lime dressing. Topped with sliced avocado. Ready in 5 minutes.
🇲🇽 Mexican-inspired Black beans 5 min assembly
Drain and rinse 1 tin black beans. Drain 1 tin sweetcorn.

Make a dressing: squeeze juice from ½ lime and mix with 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp cumin, salt, and pepper.
Toss salad with dressing. Top with sliced avocado just before serving. Ready to eat!

Dinner
Ribollita
×1
Tuscan white bean stew — cannellini beans, cavolo nero, carrot, celery, onion, tomatoes, and rosemary simmered in vegetable stock. A torn chunk of sourdough stirred in at the end to thicken it. Finished with the best extra-virgin olive oil you have.
🇮🇹 Italian Cannellini beans Vegan · High fibre
Heat olive oil in a large pan. Soften ½ onion, 1 carrot (diced), and 2 sticks celery (diced) over medium heat for 10 min.
Add 2 garlic cloves and 1 sprig of rosemary (or ½ tsp dried). Cook 1 min.
Add 1 tin chopped tomatoes and 400ml vegetable stock. Simmer 10 min.
Add 1 tin cannellini beans and a large handful of shredded cavolo nero. Simmer 10 min more until cavolo nero is tender.
Tear in a generous chunk of sourdough and stir through — it thickens the broth beautifully. Season well. Ladle into a deep bowl and drizzle with extra-virgin olive oil.

~420 kcal

Dinner
Korean Black Bean & Vegetable Stir-fry
×1
Black beans, tenderstem broccoli, edamame, and spring onions stir-fried with gochujang, tamari, sesame oil, and garlic. Served over brown rice, finished with sesame seeds and a miso dressing.
🇰🇷 Korean 🫙 Miso dressing Black beans · Edamame
Cook 75g brown rice according to packet. Make miso dressing: whisk 1 tsp white miso paste, 1 tsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sesame oil, and a splash of water together in a small bowl.
Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a wok over high heat until smoking. Add 1 garlic clove (minced), stir 30 seconds.
Add tenderstem broccoli (halved lengthways if thick). Stir-fry 3 min until bright green and slightly charred at edges.
Add edamame and black beans (drained). Add 1 tsp gochujang and 1 tbsp tamari. Toss everything together over high heat for 2 min.
Serve over brown rice. Drizzle miso dressing over the top, scatter with sesame seeds and sliced spring onion.

Dinner
Chicken Tikka Masala
×2
Chicken breast marinated in live yoghurt and spices, griddled until charred at the edges, then simmered in a rich homemade tomato sauce with garam masala, ginger, and garlic. Served with brown rice and a cooling cucumber raita.
🇮🇳 Indian 🫙 Live yoghurt (marinade + raita) Chicken breast
Marinate 2 chicken breasts: mix 4 tbsp live yoghurt, juice of ½ lemon, 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin, salt. Coat chicken and marinate at least 30 min (or overnight in fridge).
Cook 150g brown rice according to packet. Make raita: mix 4 tbsp live yoghurt with ½ cucumber (grated and squeezed dry), a handful of fresh mint, pinch of salt.
Griddle or pan-fry marinated chicken over high heat, 4–5 min each side, until nicely charred and cooked through. Rest 5 min, then slice.
In a large pan, soften 1 onion in olive oil, 10 min. Add 2 garlic cloves (minced) and 1 tsp grated fresh ginger. Cook 2 min. Add 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp smoked paprika. Stir 1 min.
Add 2 tins chopped tomatoes. Simmer 15 min until thick. Stir in 3 tbsp live yoghurt (in place of cream) and season. Add sliced chicken, warm through 5 min. Serve over rice with raita alongside.
per person
