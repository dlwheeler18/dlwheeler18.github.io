🌅 Breakfast for 2
Maple Pecan & Apple Overnight Oats
Protein 18g Fibre 9g Carbs 52g
🦠 Kefir adds live cultures; apple pectin directly feeds gut bacteria
Portions
Per person: 80g oats, 200ml oat milk, 2 tbsp kefir
Per person: 1 apple (grated), 1 handful pecans (toasted), 1 tsp maple syrup, ½ tsp cinnamon
Method
Night before: mix oats, oat milk and kefir in two jars. Stir well, seal and refrigerate. Morning: grate apple directly on top. Toast pecans in a dry pan 2–3 mins. Scatter, drizzle maple syrup, dust with cinnamon.
☀️ Lunch for 2
Greek-Style Mezze Plate
Protein 20g Fibre 12g Carbs 44g
🦠 Chickpeas, olives and olive oil deliver a powerful prebiotic and polyphenol combination
⚡ Zero cooking — assembled in 5 minutes
Portions
Per person: 3 tbsp hummus, 50g chickpeas (drained), 5 olives, 4 cherry toms, ½ cucumber, 1 slice rye sourdough, handful rocket
Drizzle of extra virgin olive oil, pinch smoked paprika, lemon wedge
Method
No cooking needed. Drain and rinse chickpeas. Halve cherry tomatoes, slice cucumber into rounds. Arrange everything on a large plate or board — hummus in the centre, chickpeas alongside, olives, tomatoes, cucumber and rocket around the edges. Drizzle olive oil over hummus, dust with paprika. Tear sourdough for dipping. Squeeze lemon over everything just before eating.
🌙 Dinner for 2
Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken Breast with Fattoush Salad
Protein 46g Fibre 10g Carbs 22g
🦠 Fresh herb diversity adds plant points; radishes are an underrated prebiotic vegetable
💡 Grill 2 extra chicken breasts tonight — refrigerate for Tuesday lunch
Portions
Per person: 1 large chicken breast (200g), 2 large handfuls mixed leaves, 4 radishes, ½ cucumber, handful cherry toms, 4 mint leaves
Marinade: juice of 1 lemon, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 garlic clove, 1 tsp dried oregano. Dressing: 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp red wine vinegar, ½ tsp sumac
Method
Mix marinade, coat both chicken breasts, rest 20 mins minimum. Heat griddle pan to high. Cook 5–6 mins each side until charred and juices run clear (75°C internal). Rest 5 mins, slice. Slice radishes, halve tomatoes, dice cucumber. Toss salad with dressing. Plate salad, lay sliced chicken over. Sumac gives a brilliant lemony tang — don't skip it.

Grilled Halloumi with Herby Quinoa Tabbouleh
Protein 36gFibre 16gCarbs 50g
🦠 Fresh herbs count as plant points; parsley and mint both actively support gut diversity
💡 Make double quinoa tabbouleh — refrigerate for Wednesday lunch
Portions
125g halloumi (sliced into 4), 60g dry quinoa, large handful flat-leaf parsley, handful mint, 3 spring onions, 10 cherry toms

Method
Cook quinoa: rinse, simmer in 120ml salted water 12 mins covered, rest 5 mins, spread on a tray to cool. Finely chop parsley, mint and spring onions. Halve tomatoes. 🌙Dinnerfor 1🌿 Veggie
Spiced Butter Bean & Roasted Pepper Shakshuka
Protein 28gFibre 12gCarbs 38g
🦠 Butter beans are the highest-fibre legume; cooked tomatoes deliver lycopene more effectively than raw
💡 Use the other half tin of butter beans for Thursday's lunch
Portions
2 eggs, ½ tin butter beans (drained), 1 roasted red pepper (jar), ½ tin chopped tomatoes, ½ red onion, 1 garlic clove
½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp smoked paprika, pinch chilli flakes, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 slice rye sourdough, handful rocket to serve
Method
Dice onion. Heat olive oil in a small pan over medium heat. Cook onion 5 mins. Add garlic and spices, stir 1 min. Slice roasted pepper and add with chopped tomatoes and butter beans. Simmer 6–8 mins until slightly thickened. Season well. Make two wells, crack an egg into each. Cover and cook on low heat 6–8 mins until whites just set, yolks still runny. Serve with rocket and sourdough.

🌙Dinnerfor 1
Sesame-Crusted Cod with Cucumber Noodle & Edamame Salad
Protein 44gFibre 12gCarbs 24g
🦠 Edamame provides complete plant protein; cucumber keeps the dish deeply hydrating
Portions
1 cod fillet (180–200g), 1 large cucumber, 80g edamame (frozen, defrosted), 2 spring onions, 1 tbsp sesame seeds
Dressing: 1 tbsp tamari, 1 tsp sesame oil, juice of ½ lime, 1 tsp honey, 1 tsp fresh ginger (grated)
Method
Press sesame seeds onto both sides of cod. Heat olive oil over medium-high. Cook cod 3–4 mins each side until sesame is golden and fish flakes. Use a vegetable peeler to make cucumber ribbons. Defrost edamame in boiling water 2 mins, drain. Slice spring onions. Whisk dressing. Toss cucumber, edamame and spring onions with dressing. Pile into a bowl, lay cod alongside.

Smoky Black Bean Tacos in Cos Lettuce Cups with Mango Salsa

🌅Breakfastfor 1🥞 Sunday special
Ricotta Pancakes with Honey, Lemon & Fresh Berries
Protein 24gFibre 8gCarbs 32g
🦠 Ricotta provides whey protein and calcium; kefir drizzle adds live cultures even on a treat breakfast
✨ Folding whipped egg whites in is the secret to the signature lightness — worth the extra bowl!
Portions
125g ricotta, 1 egg, 30g oat flour (blitz oats), 1 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp vanilla extract, pinch salt
Handful mixed berries, 1 tsp raw honey, zest of ½ lemon, optional: 1 tbsp kefir drizzled over
Method
Separate the egg. Mix ricotta, egg yolk, oat flour, baking powder, vanilla and salt until just combined — don't overwork. Whisk egg white in a separate bowl to soft peaks. Gently fold egg white into the ricotta mixture in two additions to keep them light and fluffy. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low, add a small knob of butter or drop of olive oil. Spoon 2–3 heaped tbsp per pancake. Cook 2–3 mins each side until golden and puffed. Serve with berries, honey drizzle, lemon zest and a swirl of kefir for a gut bonus.
☀️Lunchfor 1
Roasted Red Pepper Gazpacho with Rye Sourdough
Protein 8gFibre 10gCarbs 32g
🦠 Raw tomatoes and peppers retain maximum polyphenols; cold soup is deeply hydrating
⚡ Make the night before — zero effort on Sunday
Portions
3 ripe tomatoes, 1 roasted red pepper (jar), ½ cucumber, ¼ red onion, 1 garlic clove, 1–2 slices rye sourdough
2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, salt & pepper, torn basil and olive oil to serve
Method
Entirely raw. Roughly chop tomatoes, cucumber and red onion. Blitz in a blender with roasted pepper, garlic, olive oil, vinegar and seasoning until very smooth. Refrigerate at least 30 mins — or make the night before. Serve in a wide bowl, drizzle olive oil, tear basil over. Toast sourdough alongside.
🌙Dinnerfor 2
Grilled Chicken Breast with Warm Puy Lentil, Courgette & Feta Salad
Protein 52gFibre 14gCarbs 44g
🦠 Puy lentils are outstanding for gut bacteria; dressed while hot they absorb flavour and nutrients more effectively
Portions
Per person: 1 large chicken breast (200g), 80g dry puy lentils, 1 courgette, 30g feta, 2 handfuls rocket
Dressing: 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 garlic clove crushed. Marinade: olive oil, lemon zest, dried oregano
Method
Cook lentils: simmer in unsalted water 18–20 mins until just tender, drain. Slice courgettes into ribbons. Griddle or roast at 200°C 15 mins until charred. Marinate chicken in olive oil, lemon zest and oregano. Grill 5–6 mins each side, rest 5 mins. Whisk dressing. Toss warm lentils and courgette with dressing while still hot. Plate lentil salad, lay sliced chicken over, crumble feta, scatter rocket.
