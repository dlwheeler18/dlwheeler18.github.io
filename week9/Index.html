Breakfast
Raspberry & Almond Oats
×2
Ingredients (per serving: 40g oats)
40g rolled oats
125ml Plenish oat milk
½ tbsp ground flaxseed
Handful raspberries (fresh or frozen)
Small handful sliced almonds
Drizzle honey
🫙 Kefir on side Oats · Almonds
How to Make It
Measure 40g oats into a bowl. Add 125ml Plenish oat milk and ½ tbsp ground flaxseed.
Cover and refrigerate overnight, or for at least 4 hours.
Top with a handful of raspberries and sliced almonds. Drizzle with honey.
Serve with a small pot of kefir on the side.
Nutrition
Per serving: ~370 kcal
🥗
Lunch
Lentil & Roasted Vegetable Salad
×2
Ingredients
100g green lentils (dried, or use cooked)
1 small handful roasted carrots, diced
1 small handful roasted beets, diced
¼ red onion, thinly sliced
Generous handful fresh parsley, chopped
3 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp lemon juice
Salt & pepper to taste
2 slices sourdough bread
🌍 Mediterranean Green lentils
How to Make It
If making fresh: cook 100g green lentils in salted water for 20–25 min until tender. Drain and cool.
Combine lentils, roasted carrots, beets, red onion, and parsley in a bowl.
Dress with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Toss well.
Divide between 2 bowls. Serve each with a slice of sourdough.
Nutrition
Per serving: ~390 kcal

Dinner
Vietnamese-style Lentil Bowl
×2
Ingredients
150g brown rice (dry)
100g brown lentils (dry)
1 tbsp sesame oil
Juice of 1 lime
Handful fresh basil
Handful fresh mint
½ cucumber, thinly sliced
2 small handfuls pickled or grated carrots
2 spring onions, sliced
Salt to taste
🇻🇳 Vietnamese-inspired Brown rice · Lentils
How to Make It
Cook 150g brown rice and 100g brown lentils separately according to packet until tender. Drain both.
Combine rice and lentils in a bowl. Add sesame oil, lime juice, and a pinch of salt.
Top with fresh basil, mint, cucumber slices, pickled carrots, and spring onion.
Serve warm or at room temperature. Divide between 2 bowls. Drizzle with extra sesame oil if desired.

Per serving: ~470 kcal

Egg Salad with Sourdough
×1
Ingredients
2 eggs
Handful mixed salad leaves
Handful cherry tomatoes, halved
¼ cucumber, sliced
2 tbsp live yoghurt
½ tsp Dijon mustard
Squeeze lemon juice
Salt & pepper to taste
1 slice sourdough bread
🌍 Classic Eggs
How to Make It
Hard-boil 2 eggs: place in cold water, bring to boil, remove from heat, let sit 10 min. Cool and peel.
Make dressing: mix 2 tbsp live yoghurt, ½ tsp Dijon mustard, squeeze of lemon, salt, and pepper.
Halve eggs. Arrange on mixed leaves with cherry tomatoes and cucumber.
Drizzle dressing over everything. Serve with warm sourdough.
Nutrition
Per serving: ~400 kcal

Dinner
Frittata with Roasted Peppers & Herbs
×1
Ingredients
1 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, sliced
Handful spinach
½ jarred roasted red pepper, chopped
3 eggs
2 tbsp live yoghurt
Few leaves fresh basil, torn
Salt & pepper to taste
Handful mixed salad leaves
1 slice sourdough bread
🇮🇹 Italian Eggs
How to Make It
Heat olive oil in ovenproof frying pan. Soften 1 sliced onion over medium heat for 10 min.
Add a handful of spinach and cook until wilted. Add ½ roasted red pepper.
Whisk 3 eggs with 2 tbsp live yoghurt, torn basil, salt, and pepper. Pour over vegetables.
Cook on stovetop 2–3 min until edges set. Transfer to oven at 180°C for 8–10 min until centre set and top golden.
Slide onto plate. Serve with green salad and sourdough.
Nutrition
Per serving: ~450 kcal

Chickpea & Herb Salad
×1
Ingredients
1 tin chickpeas (400g)
1 tsp olive oil
½ tsp smoked paprika
Handful cherry tomatoes, halved
¼ cucumber, diced
Thin slices red onion
Handful fresh parsley
Handful fresh mint
1 tbsp tahini
1 tbsp lemon juice
1 tbsp water
Salt & pepper to taste
1 slice sourdough bread
🌍 Middle Eastern Chickpeas
How to Make It
Drain and rinse 1 tin chickpeas. Pat dry. Toss with 1 tsp olive oil, ½ tsp smoked paprika, salt, pepper.
Roast at 200°C for 20 min, shaking halfway, until golden and crispy.
Combine roasted chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, parsley, and mint.
Make dressing: whisk tahini, lemon juice, water, salt, pepper. Toss with salad. Serve with sourdough.
Nutrition
Per serving: ~410 kcal

Dinner
Chicken with Green Sauce & New Potatoes
×1
Ingredients
250g new potatoes
Large handful fresh basil
Handful fresh parsley
1 garlic clove
3 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp lemon juice
Salt & pepper to taste
1 chicken breast
Handful mixed salad leaves
🇮🇹 Italian-inspired Chicken breast
How to Make It
Boil 250g new potatoes in salted water for 15 min until tender. Drain.
Make green sauce: blend large handful basil, handful parsley, 1 garlic clove, 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp lemon juice, salt, pepper.
Season 1 chicken breast with salt and pepper. Pan-fry in a little oil for 6–7 min each side until cooked.
Slice chicken and drizzle green sauce over. Serve with boiled potatoes and a small salad dressed with lemon oil.
Nutrition
Per serving: ~480 kcal
Day total
~1,240 kcal

Dinner
Black Bean & Sweet Potato Lettuce Wraps
×1
Ingredients
250g sweet potato, cubed
1 tsp olive oil
½ tsp ground cumin
½ tsp smoked paprika
Salt & pepper to taste
1 tin black beans (400g)
¼ avocado
3 tbsp live yoghurt
Juice of ½ lime
Crisp lettuce leaves for wrapping
Handful fresh parsley
Slices fresh avocado
🇲🇽 Mexican-inspired Black beans · Sweet potato
How to Make It
Cut 250g sweet potato into small cubes. Toss with 1 tsp oil, ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp smoked paprika, salt, pepper.
Roast at 200°C for 20 min until tender and caramelised.
Drain and rinse 1 tin black beans. Add to pan with roasted sweet potato, warm through. Season to taste.
Make lime crema: blend ¼ avocado with 3 tbsp live yoghurt, juice of ½ lime, salt, pepper until smooth.
Arrange crisp lettuce leaves on plate. Fill each with warm bean-potato mix, top with lime crema, avocado slices, and parsley.
Nutrition
Per serving: ~490 kcal
