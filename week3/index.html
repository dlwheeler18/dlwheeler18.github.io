Classic Berry & Chia Overnight Oats
Ingredients
  • 50g rolled oats
  • 150ml oat milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp mixed seeds (pumpkin, sunflower)
  • 80g mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tsp raw honey
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
Method
  1. 1Combine oats, oat milk and chia seeds in a jar or bowl.
  2. 2Stir in honey and cinnamon.
  3. 3Seal and refrigerate overnight (min. 6 hrs).
  4. 4In the morning, top with berries and mixed seeds.
  5. 5Add a splash more oat milk to loosen if needed.
💡 Prep takes 3 minutes the night before — zero morning effort. Serve alongside a small pot of live yoghurt for a daily fermented food hit.

Smashed Butter Bean & Avocado Toast
Ingredients
  • 1 × 400g tin butter beans, drained
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt & pepper
  • 2 slices sourdough bread
  • Handful of rocket
  • 6 cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ cucumber, sliced
  • Pinch of chilli flakes
  • Drizzle of extra virgin olive oil
Method
  1. 1Drain and rinse butter beans. Place in a bowl.
  2. 2Add avocado, lemon juice, paprika, salt & pepper.
  3. 3Smash together with a fork — keep it chunky.
  4. 4Toast the sourdough slices.
  5. 5Spread smashed bean mix generously over each slice.
  6. 6Top with rocket, cherry tomatoes and cucumber.
  7. 7Finish with chilli flakes and a drizzle of olive oil.
💡 Make the smash the night before and refrigerate — toast the bread at lunchtime and it’s ready in 2 minutes. Sourdough is fermented, a gut-health bonus.

Chicken Breast & Chickpea Harissa Traybake
Ingredients
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 × 400g tin chickpeas, drained
  • 1 × 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp harissa paste (check label — no UPF)
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Handful of spinach to wilt in at end
  • Fresh parsley to serve
Method
  1. 1Preheat oven to 200°C.
  2. 2Mix harissa, olive oil, garlic and cumin in a large bowl.
  3. 3Add chicken breasts, chickpeas, onion, pepper. Toss to coat.
  4. 4Pour tomatoes into roasting dish. Add everything on top.
  5. 5Roast 25–30 mins until chicken is cooked through (75°C internal).
  6. 6Stir in spinach for the final 2 mins.
  7. 7Scatter parsley and serve straight from the dish.
💡 Serve each person an extra slice of sourdough alongside to boost carbs. Breast cooks faster than thigh — check at 25 mins and use a thermometer.

Banana & Walnut Overnight Oats
Ingredients
  • 50g rolled oats
  • 150ml oat milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • ½ ripe banana, mashed
  • 20g walnuts, roughly chopped
  • 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
Method

  3. 3Seal and refrigerate overnight.

  5. 5Add a splash more oat milk to loosen if needed.
💡 Mashed banana ferments very slightly overnight, adding a deeper flavour. Walnuts are rich in omega-3s and polyphenols.

Miso Noodle Soup with Greens & Edamame
Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp white miso paste
  • 500ml hot water (not boiling)
  • 80g soba (buckwheat) noodles
  • 80g frozen edamame, defrosted
  • 100g green beans, trimmed
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 garlic clove, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
Method
  1. 1Cook soba noodles per pack (usually 5 mins). Drain and rinse.
  2. 2Gently fry garlic and ginger in sesame oil for 1 min.
  3. 3Add hot water then whisk in miso — don’t boil, it kills probiotics.
  4. 4Add green beans and edamame. Simmer 2 mins.
  5. 5Add soy sauce. Taste and adjust.
  6. 6Pour over noodles in a bowl.
  7. 7Top with spring onions and sesame seeds.
💡 Never boil miso — the beneficial bacteria are heat-sensitive. One of the quickest, most gut-friendly lunches in the plan.

Dinner1 person🌿 6 plants⏱ 30 mins
Spiced Lamb Kofta with Bulgur & Yoghurt
Ingredients
  • 150g lamb mince
  • ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp coriander powder
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon, ¼ tsp smoked paprika
  • 80g bulgur wheat (dry weight)
  • ½ cucumber, diced
  • 6 cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3 tbsp natural live yoghurt
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Fresh mint, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
Method
  1. 1Cook bulgur: pour 160ml boiling water over, cover and leave 15 mins.
  2. 2Mix lamb with all spices, salt & pepper. Shape into 3–4 small koftas.
  3. 3Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat.
  4. 4Cook koftas 8–10 mins, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  5. 5Mix yoghurt with lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
  6. 6Fluff bulgur with a fork. Stir in cucumber, tomatoes, mint.
  7. 7Serve koftas on bulgur, drizzle with yoghurt.
💡 Live yoghurt contains beneficial bacteria — choose one labelled ‘live cultures’. Bulgur rehydrates hands-free while the koftas cook.

Peanut Butter & Apple Overnight Oats
Ingredients
  • 50g rolled oats
  • 150ml oat milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp natural peanut butter (peanuts only)
  • ½ apple, grated
  • 1 tsp raw honey
  • Pinch of cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp mixed seeds to top
Method
  1. 1Combine oats, oat milk and chia seeds in a jar.

💡 Grated apple adds pectin — a prebiotic fibre that feeds good gut bacteria. Natural peanut butter should list only peanuts (and maybe salt).

Teriyaki Chicken with Brown Rice & Stir-Fried Greens
Ingredients
  • 1 chicken breast, sliced into strips
  • 80g brown rice (dry weight)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp honey, 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 150g tenderstem broccoli
  • 80g frozen edamame, defrosted
  • 1 garlic clove, sliced
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds, 1 spring onion
Method
  1. 1Cook brown rice per packet (25–30 mins). Start this first.
  2. 2Mix soy, mirin, sesame oil, ginger, honey and cornflour for marinade.
  3. 3Slice chicken into strips and toss in marinade. Leave 10 mins.
  4. 4Pan-fry chicken over medium-high heat 6–8 mins until cooked through (75°C).
  5. 5Add edamame and broccoli with garlic. Stir-fry 3–4 mins.
  6. 6Serve over brown rice, pour over remaining sauce.
  7. 7Top with sesame seeds and spring onion.
💡 Slicing the breast into strips speeds up cooking and maximises marinade absorption. Don’t overcrowd the pan — single layer for a good sear.

Roasted Pepper & Butter Bean Salad with Sourdough
Ingredients
  • 1 × 400g tin butter beans, drained
  • 2 roasted red peppers (jarred, in oil)
  • Handful of rocket
  • 6 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 10g pine nuts, toasted
  • Fresh basil leaves
Method
  1. 1Drain and rinse butter beans. Pat dry.
  2. 2Slice roasted peppers into strips.
  3. 3Toast pine nuts in a dry pan 2–3 mins — watch carefully.
  4. 4Whisk olive oil, vinegar and mustard for dressing. Season.
  5. 5Toss beans, peppers, rocket and sun-dried tomatoes with dressing.
  6. 6Top with pine nuts and fresh basil.
💡 No cooking required except toasting pine nuts. Sun-dried tomatoes are rich in lycopene and polyphenols — this salad hits 8 plant points!

One-Pan Shakshuka with White Beans & Feta
Ingredients
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ × 400g tin cannellini beans, drained
  • ½ × 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • ½ red pepper, diced
  • ½ red onion, diced
  • 1 garlic clove, ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • Pinch of chilli flakes, 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 30g feta, crumbled
  • 2 tbsp natural live yoghurt to serve
  • Fresh parsley + 1 slice sourdough to serve
Method
  1. 1Heat olive oil in a frying pan. Fry onion and pepper 5 mins.
  2. 2Add garlic, cumin, paprika and chilli. Cook 1 min.
  3. 3Pour in tomatoes and beans. Simmer 10 mins until thickened. Season.
  4. 4Make 2 wells in the sauce. Crack in the eggs.
  5. 5Cover and cook on low 6–8 mins — whites set, yolks runny.
  6. 6Crumble feta over the top.
  7. 7Scatter parsley, serve with yoghurt and sourdough.
💡 White beans boost fibre to ~15g and add extra plant-based protein. One pan, minimal washing up. Sauce can be made ahead.

Slow-Cooked Beef & Root Vegetable Stew
Ingredients
  • 300g beef braising steak, diced
  • 2 medium carrots, chunked
  • 2 parsnips, chunked
  • 1 large onion, roughly chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 400ml beef stock (low sodium)
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée, 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried thyme, 1 bay leaf
  • Handful of savoy cabbage, shredded
  • Wholegrain bread or mash to serve
Method
  1. 1Heat oil in a deep pan. Brown beef in batches 3–4 mins. Set aside.
  2. 2Fry onion, celery and garlic 5 mins until softened.
  3. 3Add tomato purée and stir 1 min.
  4. 4Return beef. Add stock, carrots, parsnips, thyme, bay leaf.
  5. 5Bring to a boil, reduce heat. Cover and simmer 40 mins.
  6. 6Add savoy cabbage for final 5 mins. Season to taste.
  7. 7Serve with wholegrain bread or mashed potato.
💡 Start this at 6pm for a relaxed Friday dinner. Leftovers are even better the next day — the flavours deepen overnight.

Mushroom & Spinach Omelette with Sourdough
Ingredients
  • 4 large eggs
  • 150g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
  • 100g baby spinach
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper
  • 2 slices sourdough
  • Fresh chives, snipped
Method
  1. 1Heat 1 tbsp oil. Fry mushrooms and garlic 4–5 mins.
  2. 2Add spinach and cook until wilted. Season. Remove from pan.
  3. 3Beat 2 eggs per person with salt & pepper.
  4. 4Heat remaining oil. Pour in eggs, tilting pan to spread.
  5. 5When almost set, add the generous mushroom and spinach filling to one half.
  6. 6Fold over — the filling keeps it moist without needing cheese.
  7. 7Serve with toasted sourdough and chives.
💡 Mushrooms contain beta-glucans which support immune function. Doubling the mushrooms and spinach shifts the macro balance and adds more plant points.

Black Bean & Sweet Potato Bake with Sourdough
Ingredients
  • 1 × 400g tin black beans, drained
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, diced (1cm)
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 1 red pepper, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp oregano
  • 150ml passata + 1 tbsp chipotle paste
  • 60g cheddar, grated
  • Natural live yoghurt + lime + parsley to serve
  • 4 thick slices sourdough, to serve
Method
  1. 1Preheat oven to 190°C.
  2. 2Roast sweet potato with 1 tbsp oil, cumin and paprika for 20 mins.
  3. 3Fry onion, pepper and garlic in remaining oil 5 mins.
  4. 4Add beans, roasted sweet potato and oregano. Mix well.
  5. 5Mix passata with chipotle paste. Pour into an oven dish.
  6. 6Spoon bean mixture over the sauce. Sprinkle cheese on top.
  7. 7Bake 15–20 mins until bubbling and golden.
  8. 8Serve with yoghurt, lime, parsley and toasted sourdough.
💡 Deconstructed enchilada — same flavours, scooped onto sourdough instead. Completely UPF-free.

Buckwheat Pancakes with Berries & Maple Syrup
Ingredients
  • 150g buckwheat flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 250ml oat milk
  • 1 tsp baking powder, pinch of salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g mixed berries
  • 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
  • Natural live yoghurt to serve
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil for frying
Method
  1. 1Whisk eggs, oat milk and vanilla together.
  2. 2Add buckwheat flour, baking powder and salt. Whisk to a smooth batter.
  3. 3Rest 5 mins.
  4. 4Heat coconut oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  5. 5Pour small ladles of batter. Cook 2–3 mins per side.
  6. 6Keep warm in oven at 80°C while cooking remaining pancakes.
  7. 7Serve stacked with berries, yoghurt and maple syrup.
💡 Buckwheat is gluten-free, rich in fibre and polyphenols, with a lower GI than standard flour. A proper weekend treat.

Roasted Tomato & White Bean Soup
Ingredients
  • 400g vine tomatoes, halved
  • 1 × 400g tin cannellini beans, drained
  • 1 red onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves (unpeeled)
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 500ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp dried thyme, 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
  • Handful of fresh basil
  • Sourdough to serve
Method
  1. 1Preheat oven to 200°C.
  2. 2Place tomatoes and onion cut-side up on a tray. Tuck in garlic.
  3. 3Drizzle with 2 tbsp olive oil, thyme, salt & pepper. Roast 25 mins.
  4. 4Squeeze garlic from skins. Transfer veg to a blender with stock.
  5. 5Blend until smooth. Pour into a pan with beans.
  6. 6Simmer 5 mins. Add balsamic and season.
  7. 7Serve with fresh basil, drizzle of olive oil and sourdough.
💡 Roasting concentrates the tomatoes’ lycopene and polyphenol content. Add a swirl of live yoghurt for a probiotic boost.

Roast Chicken Breast with Brown Rice & Roasted Roots
Ingredients
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 160g brown rice (dry weight)
  • 2 medium carrots, halved lengthways
  • 2 medium parsnips, halved lengthways
  • 1 small beetroot, quartered
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp rosemary, 1 tsp thyme
  • 3 garlic cloves, juice of 1 lemon
  • Steamed savoy cabbage to serve
Method
  1. 1Preheat oven to 190°C.
  2. 2Toss all root veg with 2 tbsp olive oil, rosemary, thyme, salt and pepper.
  3. 3Rub chicken with garlic, lemon juice, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt & pepper.
  4. 4Place veg in a large roasting tin. Nestle chicken on top.
  5. 5Roast 25–30 mins until chicken reaches 75°C internal. Rest 5 mins.
  6. 6Cook brown rice per packet (25–30 mins — start first).
  7. 7Steam savoy cabbage for final 5 mins. Serve everything together.
💡 Brown rice is a great source of fibre and B vitamins. Start it before the oven goes on as it takes 25–30 mins.
